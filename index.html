FAQ

      
Questions we get a lot

      

        Don't see yours? Call or
        text (949) 731-4378 and just ask.
      

    


    


      

        
          
How do I book?

          
        
        

          
Use the booking form — it takes about a minute. It opens an email to us with your details already written out; you just hit send. Then we'll call or text to confirm your arrival window and the rate. Prefer to skip the form? Call or text (949) 731-4378.

        

      


      

        
          
What areas do you cover?

          
        
        

          
Orange County, California — from Seal Beach and La Habra down through Irvine and out to San Clemente. If you're right on the county line or moving just outside it, ask us and we'll give you a straight answer.

        

      


      

        
          
How much does it cost?

          
        
        

          
Moving and labor-only work are billed hourly, and both rates are posted in the pricing section. Junk removal is priced by how much there is rather than by the hour, so send a photo and we'll quote it. Either way, we go over anything that affects the total before we start.

        

      


      

        
          
Can you haul junk away during my move?

          
        
        

          
Often, yes. Mention it in the notes on the booking form — tell us roughly what's going and we'll let you know what we can fit into the same visit.

        

      


      

        
          
Do you do piano, safe, or other heavy items?

          
        
        

          
Tell us upfront. There's a spot on the booking form to flag pianos, safes, gym equipment and similar. We'd rather know in advance than find out on the day — it changes how many people we bring and how long it takes.

        

      


      

        
          
Is there anything you can't take?

          
        
        

          
Hazardous material — paint, solvents, chemicals, propane tanks and the like — generally needs special disposal and can't just go on the truck. Ask us before your appointment and we'll tell you what we can and can't take.

        

      


      

        
          
What if something isn't done right?

          
        
        

          
Say something before we leave. That's our satisfaction guarantee — furniture in the wrong room, a box that needs to go back upstairs, a spot that needs another sweep. We'd much rather fix it on the spot than have you find it after we've driven off.

        

      


      

        
          
What are your hours?

          
        
        

          
We work by appointment rather than fixed store hours. Pick the date you want on the booking form, tell us whether morning or afternoon suits you better, and we'll confirm a window that works for both of us.
